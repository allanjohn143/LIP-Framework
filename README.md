## Commercial Use: LICENSE REQUIRED

**What counts as commercial use:**
- Implementation within for-profit companies
- Consulting or training services using LIP Framework
- Integration into commercial products or services
- Use by organizations generating revenue
- Any application primarily directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation

**Why a commercial license is required:**
The NonCommercial clause in CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 explicitly prohibits commercial use without separate permission. Organizations using LIP commercially must obtain proper licensing to:
- Ensure legal compliance
- Support framework development and evolution
- Access implementation expertise and support
- Maintain quality standards through verification
- Contribute to subsidizing non-commercial access

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I try LIP before committing to a commercial license?**

A: Yes. For serious inquiries, we can arrange a pilot engagement or initial consultation to assess fit. Contact us to discuss.

**Q: What if we're a nonprofit but generate some revenue?**

A: Many nonprofits generate revenue but remain mission-driven. If your primary purpose is not commercial advantage, you likely qualify for free access. Contact us to clarify.

**Q: Can we implement LIP ourselves using just the public documentation?**

A: Legally, you need a commercial license. Practically, self-implementation often fails because formation practices cannot be learned from text alone. We strongly recommend implementation partnership for success.

**Q: What if we can't afford the commercial license?**

A: Contact us. For organizations doing important work with limited resources, we may offer payment plans, reduced rates, or pro bono access. We want LIP implemented well, not just widely.

**Q: How do you verify commercial use?**

A: We monitor public mentions, job postings, consulting firm websites, LinkedIn activity, and receive community reports. We prefer proactive licensing over reactive enforcement.

**Q: Can we get equity instead of paying cash licensing fees?**

A: For certain partnerships (especially AGI labs or high-growth technology companies), equity arrangements can be negotiated. Contact us to discuss.

**Q: Does the license cover subsidiaries and affiliates?**

A: Typically covers the licensed entity only. Multi-entity organizations need appropriate licensing structure. Details in contract.

**Q: Can we white-label the framework?**

A: No. Attribution is required under all licenses. However, we can discuss co-branding arrangements in custom licensing agreements.

**Q: What happens if we violate the license terms?**

A: License terminates. You must cease using the framework commercially. Legal action may follow. We prefer working with you to maintain compliance rather than litigation.
